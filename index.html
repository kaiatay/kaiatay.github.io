About

Latvian-American, undergraduate aerospace engineering student passionate about advancing rocket propulsion and launch system reliability through testing and development. Currently the Payload Strucutres team lead in the NC State’s High-Powered Rocketry Team, focusing on payload system design and testing. Contributing to the NCSU BEFAST Lab's Baby RDRE and Bladeless Turbine Projects. Experienced in propulsion diagnostics, drone development, mechanical system restoration and structural response analysis, with a strong foundation in aerodynamics and control systems. Driven to apply hands-on engineering and analytical skills toward developing efficient, reusable propulsion technologies that expand access to space.
